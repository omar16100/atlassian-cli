## Takeaways
- Backlink profile is essentially greenfield (1 domain / 1 link). Link building is the biggest lever.
- SERP is winnable: no term is locked by a single dominant independent CLI; github.com (repo pages), Appfire (paid), Reddit, and package registries (crates.io, npm, pypi, snapcraft) hold the top spots. atlassiancli.com already ranks for all four but needs to move from page 2 to page 1.
- Priority: improve `jira cli` (currently pos 40, the highest-value/highest-competition term) and consolidate `confluence cli` / `atlassian cli` / `bitbucket cli` (all near pos 14-17, closest to page 1).
