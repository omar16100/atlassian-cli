## Notes for blog angles

- Core product terms `jira cli` / `cli jira` / `jira-cli` (1900 vol, KD 6-10) are the flagship low-difficulty targets.

- `jira api` (2900, KD 13) and `jira rest api` (1000, KD 7) are high-value how-to/dev content, informational intent.

- Automation cluster (`jira automation` 1300 KD 17, plus rules/examples/triggers) is broad and mostly low-KD.

- JQL cluster: `jira jql` (390 KD 14), `jql query` (480 KD 6) good for query-cheatsheet content.
